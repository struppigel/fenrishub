Bad regex rules

Regex rules accepted by re2 are evaluated in linear time and cannot backtrack — they are safe. The danger zone is the fallback bucket: rules whose pattern re2 rejected (backreferences, lookaround, unsupported syntax) get compiled with stdlib re and evaluated one-by-one. Those are the only patterns that can hang a Gunicorn worker on a large log.

Each fallback rule below is timed against 9 short adversarial inputs (long repetitions, mixed chars, fake paths). A rule that takes more than a few milliseconds on a 200-character input is almost certainly catastrophic.
